# Content Model and Placeholder Policy

Required areas: company identity, hero proposition, company overview, categories, product records, applications, services/support, FAQ, contact, footer.

Use explicit development placeholders: `[COMPANY_NAME]`, `[APPROVED_TAGLINE]`, `[PRODUCT_NAME]`, `[VERIFIED_SPEC_REQUIRED]`, `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]`, `[PHONE_IF_APPROVED]`, `[OFFICIAL_ADDRESS]`, `[CERTIFICATION_IF_VERIFIED]`.

Never invent facts. Warn during development if required public fields remain placeholders; do not publish placeholder text as polished factual copy.

Copy style: concise, confident, technical, human. Avoid unsupported claims such as “world-leading,” efficiency guarantees, customer names, or certification claims. FAQ topics can cover product selection, inquiry requirements, service availability, documentation, and support—but answers require approved company policy.
